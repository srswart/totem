## Objective

Calibration currently has nowhere to happen. The only estate with the real
embedder in front of it is **the dogfood estate**, which is also the one
holding real working memories — so every measurement both contaminates the
thing we depend on and is contaminated by it.

ADV-CORE-008 demonstrated both directions in a single afternoon: measuring
the deployment reinforced its records, and the records' accumulated history
(some of it generated by the console browsing them) was what made the
measurement hard to interpret.

This advance builds a place to calibrate that is **separate from the estate
we rely on, disposable, and reproducible.**

## Behavioral Change

- A calibration estate can be **created** with a named corpus version
  (ADV-STORE-009) loaded into it, against the real embedder.
- It can be **measured** without touching the dogfood estate, and without
  reinforcing anything (ADV-GATEWAY-017).
- It can be **discarded and rebuilt** from the same corpus version, so a
  before/after comparison starts from an identical state rather than from
  whatever the last run left behind. This is the property ADV-CORE-008 most
  needed and did not have.
- It can be rebuilt from a **modified** corpus, so "what happens if the
  economics differ / the clusters are tighter / this category is added" is a
  question that can be asked in minutes.
- Multiple projects can be enrolled and stay isolated. The store layer
  already supports this and is tested — `corpus.rs` seeds two fictional repos
  and the leak-bait pairs prove neither owner's chain returns the other's
  copy — so this is an operational advance, not an architectural one.

## The reset problem, which is the interesting part

**A corpus cannot be wiped in place.** Episodic rows are append-only at the
schema level (`schema.rs`'s `memory_episodic_no_delete` event refuses
`DELETE` outright), and that invariant is load-bearing — it should not be
weakened to make testing convenient. So "reset" has to mean **building a
fresh container**, and the advance must choose which:

1. **A separate SurrealDB namespace or database** in the same deployment.
   Cheapest, and the engine lock (DEP-001) is already held by the gateway, so
   nothing new contends for it. Needs the gateway to address more than one.
2. **A separate deployment** (a `totem-calib` app). Strongest isolation, no
   risk to the dogfood estate at all, but a second thing to deploy and pay
   for — and ADV-INFRA-006 has to land first or each iteration costs a full
   rebuild.
3. **A local instance** with the real embedder. Fastest loop, no deployment
   at all — but it is then not measuring the deployed configuration, which is
   where every defect this project has found actually lived.

**Choose one and record why the others were rejected.** Note that (3) is the
tempting one and that the project's own history argues against trusting it:
ADV-GATEWAY-013, ADV-CLI-002, ADV-GATEWAY-015, ADV-GATEWAY-010 and
ADV-STORE-008 were all defects that in-process testing structurally could not
reach (`docs/overnight-experiment/log.md`).

## Scope and Boundaries

**In scope:** the calibration estate's creation, seeding, teardown, and the
operator path to run all three; the enrolment of the calibration project; the
runbook entry.

**Out of scope:** the corpus artifact itself (ADV-STORE-009); the evaluation
that runs against it (ADV-CORE-005); non-reinforcing reads
(ADV-GATEWAY-017), which this advance **depends on** — seeding and measuring
an estate that reinforces itself would rebuild the problem.

## Risk + Rollback

- Risk (`deployment`): a calibration corpus reaching the dogfood estate.
  `GENERATOR_TAG` makes synthetic records identifiable after the fact, which
  is a mitigation and not a prevention — the separation must be structural.
- Risk: a calibration estate that drifts from the deployed configuration and
  therefore measures something nobody runs. Whatever option is chosen, record
  what differs from production and why it is acceptable.
- Rollback: discard the estate; nothing in the dogfood path changes.

## Evidence

- [ ] deployment:executed — create, seed, measure, discard, recreate, and
      show the **second measurement matches the first**. Reproducibility is
      the whole claim, and an unrepeated measurement does not demonstrate it.
- [ ] tests:integration — the calibration project's records are unreachable
      from a dogfood reader's scope chain.

## Check for Understanding

1. Episodic rows cannot be deleted. Why is that invariant worth keeping even
   though it makes calibration harder, and what does "reset" therefore have
   to mean?
2. A local instance with the real embedder would give the fastest iteration.
   Name two defects from this project's history that such a setup would have
   missed.
3. This advance depends on ADV-GATEWAY-017. What specifically goes wrong if
   the calibration estate is seeded and measured while reads still reinforce?
4. `GENERATOR_TAG` is called a mitigation rather than a prevention. What is
   the distinction, and what would a prevention look like?
5. The evidence requires measuring twice with a teardown between. What claim
   does the second measurement support that the first cannot?
